TunXBox is an open-source NekoBox/SagerNet fork for Android TV and phones, powered by sing-box.

• Remote-friendly TV interface and phone interface
• Connection profiles and groups
• Local configuration exchange with QR codes and a browser
• Routing datasets and a local dashboard

Bring your own compatible servers or subscription. TunXBox does not provide VPN service or subscription accounts. Feature availability depends on your configuration, device and installed version.

Source code, release notes and known limitations are available on GitHub. Back up your settings before reinstalling or changing signing channels.
